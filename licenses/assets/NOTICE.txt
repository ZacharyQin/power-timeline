This layered visualization includes exterior shell geometry adapted from Valve's
Steam Deck surface CAD released as steamdeck_*_20220202. The interior geometry
is a project-created illustrative reconstruction of the OLED layout, not official
OLED manufacturing CAD. Changes include an illustrative cutaway, reconstructed
internal parts, material colours, orthographic projection and transparent layers.
No thermal simulation or measured internal geometry is claimed.
